# application overview

## features

- **shift management:** managers can create, edit, and assign work shifts to employees.
- **availability:** employees can submit the days and times they are available to work.
- **schedule:** employees can view their upcoming shifts in one organized calendar.
- **work hour tracking:** employees can track their total working hours.
- **shift notifications:** the app sends notifications when shifts are added or changed.
- **employee management:** managers can organize employees and view their assigned shifts.
